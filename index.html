OUR STORY

Hot Plate 2.0 brings the heart of South African cuisine to Sunninghill with a modern, vibrant twist. Our menu celebrates familiar local flavours through shareable plates, crafted with fresh ingredients and bold seasoning.

Enjoy a relaxed dining experience complemented by signature cocktails inspired by South African culture. With great food, warm service, and a dynamic atmosphere, Hot Plate 2.0 is your go-to destination for authentic taste and effortless comfort.
